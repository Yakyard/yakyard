# Getting help with Yakyard

Yakyard is free. There's no company selling support, no help desk and no SLA. Here's where help lives, in the order to try it.

1. **Setting it up?** [Set up Yakyard, free](docs/SETUP.md), and its [When something fails](docs/SETUP.md#when-something-fails) section. Most problems are in there.
2. **Using it?** [How to use Yakyard](docs/GUIDE.md), or the **?** button inside the app.
3. **A question?** The [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md) first, then [ask a question](https://github.com/yakyard/yakyard/issues/new?template=question.yml).
4. **Something broken?** [Report a bug](https://github.com/yakyard/yakyard/issues/new?template=bug.yml). Run `npx wrangler tail` while you repeat the problem and paste the red lines.
5. **A security problem?** Not in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
6. **A commercial license?** [Ask a question](https://github.com/yakyard/yakyard/issues/new?template=question.yml) and say so in the title.

Answers come from the maintainers and other people running Yakyard, when they have time. Be specific and patient, and you'll usually get further.

Cloudflare account, billing or outage questions go to Cloudflare.
